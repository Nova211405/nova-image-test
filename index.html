About NOVA

A concise introduction to the project.

NOVA (NVA) is a digital token deployed on BNB Smart Chain. Its token model is based on a fixed total supply of 21,000,000 NOVA and a published allocation structure. This website is informational only. It does not promise returns and is not investment advice.

FOUNDER ALLOCATION

Vesting Schedule

Founder allocation: 5,000,000 NOVA. This includes 2,000,000 NOVA initially unlocked and 3,000,000 NOVA vesting over 36 months (3 years). This schedule is separate from the public sale cap.
